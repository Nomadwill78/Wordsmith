How a run plays

  1. 01

    Pick a room

    Every word on the grid is a room. Words that cross ones you've solved open up. Choose your path through the floor.

  2. 02

    Spell to strike

    Each correct letter hits harder than the last. Clear a room without a mistake to build your combo. At three, every hit lands at 1.5×.

  3. 03

    Break the seal

    Solve 60% of the floor to wake the boss, always the longest word. Crossings won't save you. Bosses must be spelled in full.

  4. 04

    Descend and grow

    Five floors, each bigger than the last. Bank your Ink between runs and buy permanent perks in the Scriptorium.

Questions

Is the full game different from the demo?

The demo is today's first two floors. The full game has five floors per run, unlimited new runs, save and resume, and the Scriptorium perk shop that carries progress between runs.

Do I need to be good at crosswords?

No. Clues are straightforward, and crossing letters give you hints. The challenge is spelling cleanly under pressure, not trivia.

Is it on Steam?

Not right now. It's sold only here so the developer keeps more of each sale and can email buyers directly about updates.
